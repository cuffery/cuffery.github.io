## 💡 So... What *Is* This Site For?

This site is where I:
- Share lessons learned (and mistakes made) so future-me doesn’t make them again
- Write semi-coherent thoughts about the ever-chaotic world of data engineering
- Document side projects that don’t fit neatly in a README
- Explore new tools, frameworks, and data things I find interesting


If you're into weird little experiments, pipelines that almost make sense, or just want to peek behind the curtain of what data folks do when no one is looking — you're in the right place.
